# Assistance disclosure

This project was developed with extensive assistance from Codex and several separate AI reviewer agents at the student's request. The agents helped research the dataset/model choice, refine the experiment, acquire and audit data, write Python and notebook code, prepare visualizations and documentation, and review implementation and analysis. Separate agents are AI reviewers, not independent human assessors or a guarantee of marks.

The near-duplicate screening decisions were made by AI-assisted visual inspection of candidate contact sheets and selected full-resolution image pairs. They concern image duplication only. They do not establish agricultural label correctness, farmer/plant independence or demographic fairness. The candidate pairs, decisions and limitations are preserved under `results/data_audit/`.

Reported experimental measurements come from actual executed code and saved outputs, not language-model estimates. Data acquisition and audit, model implementation checks, full-epoch GPU pilots, all six training runs, final test evaluation, synthetic sensitivity checks and analysis were executed locally on 23 October 2026. Evidence and limitations are recorded in `results/README.md` and the saved run files. The separate results-review notebook was executed to display these saved artifacts. A Colab-compatible reproduction notebook is supplied, but Colab execution must not be claimed unless it is separately performed and recorded.

The student has not yet verified or adopted every generated artifact as their own final submission. Before submission, the student must understand and check the code and results, review the report and citations, comply with the course's AI-assistance and authorship rules, and be able to explain the experimental design, limitations and findings in the presentation. This disclosure does not itself establish that the course permits a particular level of AI assistance.

Dataset and pretrained-model attribution remain separate from AI assistance. Their original sources and license information are linked in `README.md` and should be retained in the final report and repository.
